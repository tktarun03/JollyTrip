# Changelog

Generated by `python3 build.py` from `CHANGELOG` in `src/b-data.js`, which is also what the app shows under More → What’s new. Edit it there.

## 3.7 · 2026-11-06

- Save backup makes a file you can keep in Files or iCloud Drive, and Restore from backup can read it back. Today reminds you when your last backup is more than a week old.
- Add to Calendar: the whole trip from Plan → Bookings, or one booking from its sheet or pass. Check-in, check-out, flights, balances due and timed plans come with alerts.
- Add to Apple Wallet now uses a pass you’ve just attached, before you save the booking.

## 3.6 · 2026-11-05

- Directions: tap the arrow next to a place or booking and choose Apple Maps, Google Maps or Waze, for driving, walking or transit. Save a favourite in More → Maps and wallet.
- Add to Apple Wallet on every booking: uses the airline’s or hotel’s own pass if attached, or makes one with the free WalletWallet service.
- Bookings have an address, read from pasted or scanned confirmations when possible.

## 3.5 · 2026-11-05

- Travel wallet: every booking as a pass with its code as a QR code, offline. Open it from Plan → Bookings or any booking.
- Add the airline’s or hotel’s own Apple Wallet pass (.pkpass) to Apple Wallet, or open their Google Wallet link.
- “+ Add a wallet…” in the spend, booking and CSV import forms, so you can add a card or cash wallet without leaving the form.

## 3.4 · 2026-11-03

- Face ID or fingerprint can be turned on straight from More → Security or your profile. Without a PIN, it walks you through setting one first, and new PINs offer Face ID right away.

## 3.3 · 2026-11-03

- The app locks itself after 5 minutes without a touch, click or keystroke (with a PIN or Face ID set up). The time follows your Lock the app setting.
- Pinch and double-tap zoom are turned off. Use More → Appearance → Text size instead.

## 3.2 · 2026-11-03

- Paste a booking’s confirmation text to fill in its details.
- Bookings get a picture: the hotel photo from your PDF, a photo you attach, or a drawn picture for each kind of booking.
- Messages now slide up from the bottom and have a close button.

## 3.1 · 2026-11-03

- Version history: your trips are saved automatically while you make changes, before big changes, and whenever you choose. Restore any version in one tap.
- Undo straight after deleting, importing or restoring.
- App lock with a PIN, plus Face ID or fingerprint unlock, in More → Security.
- Attach booking confirmations as PDFs or photos. JollyTrip reads the code, dates and total, and can read the amount from a spend’s receipt photo.
- Manage your profile, including your own picture.
- A refresh button, and sheets whose title stays put while the content scrolls.
